### Scope of Variables

~Scope: region of a program where a variable is accessible and can be used.

~Block Scope: declared within a block of code (within curly braces '{}'), they are accessible
only within that block and its nested blocks.

~Function Scope: accessible within that function, including any nested blocks.

~File Scope (Global): declared outside of any function or block, can be accessed within the entire file.

&*Example is scope.c

### Lifetime of Variables

~Lifetime: period during which a variable exists in memory.

~Block Scope Lifetime: limited to the block in which they are defined.

~Function Scope Lifetime: extends from the moment they are declared to the end of the function.

~Global Scope Lifetime: the entire duration of the program.

&*Example is lifetime.c
